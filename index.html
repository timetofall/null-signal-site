NULL SIGNAL

Null Signal drops players into a world shattered by an AI uprising. Each biome is a self-contained ~3×3 km instance—dense, handcrafted environments connected through a boss-gated progression system inspired by Valheim. Clear the area’s threat, defeat its guardian, and push deeper into increasingly hostile territory.

The world fights back. Drawing from The Forest, enemy AI reacts to player success—the more you build, harvest, and thrive, the more aggressive and coordinated threats become. AXIS surveillance drones patrol overhead, and the deeper you integrate with AXIS technology, the more the network tracks and manipulates you.

Loot matters. Weapons and gear drop with randomized stat rolls, bringing action RPG depth to the survival loop. Every find is a potential upgrade, every crafting session a chance to push your build further.

COMPONENT LAYER

Real boards, real corrosion

Dense PCBs and solder—straight from the camera. This is the “computer” half of the mood: metal, glass, and traces that used to mean something.
